Approach

How the system works

We start from physical and supply-side constraints — capacity gaps, import dependencies, bottlenecked products — and work outward to the companies positioned on the right side of them, rather than starting from stock charts or factor screens.

Quantitative screens only generate candidates; every inclusion, sizing and exclusion is an explicit, written judgment logged before outcomes are known, in an append-only decision log.

The cycle runs monthly: each report is strictly point-in-time, decisions are recorded with their rationale and the conditions that would flip them, and forward returns are published here against a same-window benchmark.
