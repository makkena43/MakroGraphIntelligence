opinion, various favourable judgements, and amendments in the Income-tax Act, 1961, this will not have
any significant impact on the Company's financial position.

commercial production on 4 August 2023. The installed production capacity of the new plant is 1500 MT
per day.

lakhs, and loss for nine-month period is Rs. 18,101 lakhs emerging out of initial stabilization period and
low Ammonia prices globally. During the current quarter the Ammonia Plant has achieved a stable
capacity utilisation.
With this facility, the Group will significantly reduce its dependency on imported ammonia, which will
also enhance operational efficiencies.
