approval to sub-divide/split 1 (one) Equity shares of the Company of Rs.10/- each, fully paid-up
into 5 (five) equity shares of face value of Rs.2/- each, fully paid-up by alteration of Capital Clause
of the Memorandum of Association of the Company, subject to the approval of the members of
the Company to be sought by means of Postal Ballot, including such approvals from regulatory /
