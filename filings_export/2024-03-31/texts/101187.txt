Company Overview
• Associated Alcohols and Breweries Ltd. (AABL)
was founded by the late Shri Bhagwati Prasad
Kedia in 1989.
• It is run today under the stewardship of his two
sons, Mr. Anand Kedia and Mr. Prasann Kedia.
• AABL is one of the largest distillers in India with a
state-of-the-art integrated alcoholic beverages
manufacturing facility located in Central India
near Indore, Madhya Pradesh.
• The
company
manufactures
premium
Extra
Neutral Alcohol (ENA)/triple distilled/rectified
spirit, IMIL and IMFL.
• AABL has diversified business segments, from
manufacturing and supply of ENA to contract
manufacturing for Diageo-USL, to manufacturing
and marketing of licensed brands, as well as IMIL
and 6 IMFL brands.
