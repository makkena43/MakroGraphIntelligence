Pearl Global Industries Limited founded in 1987, is a leading apparel manufacturer, offering
end-to-end sustainable solutions to the fashion industry. We have a multinational presence across
eight countries, India, Indonesia, Bangladesh, Vietnam, for manufacturing and USA, Spain,
Hong Kong and UK for client servicing and designing.
Our diversified product offering across categories includes knitted tees, polos, and dresses,
woven shirts, blouses, and dresses, denim jeans, outerwear jackets, active performance wear,
and athleisure wear. And we produce for men, women and toddlers. Our 75 plus designers across
these eight locations leave the journey for us from concept to the finished product.
Pearl Global has a well-diversified and derisked manufacturing base with 22 dedicated
manufacturing units. Our total capacity to manufacture is around 82 million units per year.
Some of the key clientele includes Kohl's, Macy's, Tommy Hilfiger, Gap, Old Navy, Next,
Nordstrom, Muji amongst others. In other words, we are a one-stop solution for the needs of
retailer.

working towards our mission to exceed customer and shareholder’s expectations. We do this by
