## Decision protocol

- Discovery routes: new earnings engine, demand-backed expansion, or identifiable profit recovery.
- Research labels: ADVANCE, WATCH, NOT SUPPORTED, INSUFFICIENT SOURCE (and OUTSIDE MODEL if applicable).
- Investment labels: READY FOR CONSIDERATION, WAIT (price or named milestone), REJECT, RESEARCH INCOMPLETE. No label is a current buy recommendation.
- Construct rough three-year downside/base/upside earnings and valuation sensitivities where financial evidence supports them. Analyst assumptions are not management guidance. Use earnings attributable to shareholders and allow for known dilution; distinguish standalone/consolidated and non-controlling interests.
- Illustrative hurdle fixed now: 15% annualized base-case price return over three years, before dividends/costs/tax; assess downside separately. This is a pilot convention, not a personalized required return or a validated optimal cutoff. No mechanical buy follows solely from crossing it.
- An investment-ready label additionally requires credible demand/execution, financeability, acceptable governance evidence, and no unresolved critical input. Missing evidence is not silently scored as a failure or success.
- Outcome evaluation: first trading close after each cutoff; 12/24/36-month anniversary closes on or before anniversaries. Compare matched dates with NIFTYBEES and JUNIORBEES as market-price benchmark proxies. If a directly comparable smaller-cap history is unavailable, disclose this rather than claim size-adjusted alpha. Verify splits, bonuses, rights, mergers and security identity. Price returns exclude dividends, trading costs and taxes; do not call them total returns.
- Record fixed-horizon performance of every sampled company, including waits/rejections. This is not a dynamically managed portfolio backtest. A price-wait label will not be retroactively treated as a purchase at a later lower price.

### Process verdict

1. Useful research triage, not proven alpha: Mold-Tek was the strongest expansion candidate; Balaji caution helped; CEAT and GIPCL were missed under the frozen initial decisions. No fully cleared buy portfolio was constructed, so do not claim a strategy win rate or portfolio return.
2. Mold-Tek beat NIFTYBEES at 36 months but lagged JUNIORBEES by approximately 18.8 percentage points. Its approximately two-year doubling is not proof of durable three-year benchmark outperformance.
3. Keep separate expansion and recovery/value paths. Future tests should reassess WAIT cases quarterly against named milestones, rather than permanently excluding them. This is a proposed next-test rule, not backdated to these results.
4. Make normalized EPS, dilution and valuation stress tests mandatory; do not use capacity growth or management revenue aspirations as a proxy for shareholder earnings growth.
5. Review holdings when execution weakens or price substantially outruns a supported earnings case. This pilot did not test a sell rule; selling at the observed best horizon would be hindsight.
6. Before automating or using real money, test the fixed process on additional untouched cases and complete governance/customer diligence for actual candidates. Six cases, incomplete disclosure coverage, search contamination and judgmental multiples prevent a reliability claim. In deference to the user's credit budget, stop this pilot here; do not expand it automatically.
